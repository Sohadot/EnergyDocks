Energydocks.com

The point where a moving system stops to take on energy.

Ships at berth, robots returning to base, trucks and yard equipment on a depot cycle. Each one approaches, connects, draws or exchanges power, identifies itself, and leaves again. The equipment differs. The sequence does not.

Where this happens

  • Marine Shore connection at berth, port electrification, autonomous surface and subsea vessels returning to a docking point.
  • Robotic Drones, warehouse and field robots that dock, charge and resume a task without a person in the loop.
  • Fleet Depot and on-route charging for buses, heavy trucks, port vehicles and yard equipment, increasingly through automated connection rather than a handheld plug.

About the name

Energydocks.com is a plural, sector-neutral name for this moment of connection. It is held as a strategic domain and is not affiliated with any existing product or company using a similar term.
